**When you get a pick**
- **After the draft:** your new manager offers a pick in his office (3 choices; 2 if you went after round 1)
- **At college:** one pick when you join the team; it leans toward your school's focus (2 choices, 3 with a full offer)
- **Every spring:** your agent offers a pick before camp, sized by last season
  - Hitters: .800+ OPS = 3 choices, .700+ = 2, otherwise 1
  - Pitchers: 3.00 ERA or better = 3, 4.00 or better = 2, otherwise 1
  - Under 150 PAs or 50 IP = 2

**Earn your spot** (an earned quirk is always in the offer)
- **Situational Hitter:** hit .333+ with runners in scoring position (50+ ABs)
- **Table Setter:** 30+ SBs
- **Stopper:** 30+ saves, or 12+ quality starts
- **Homebody / Road Warrior:** hit .320+ (150+ ABs) or post a 2.50 ERA or better (50+ IP as a starter, 25+ in relief) at home / on the road
- **Outlier I / II:** 99 velocity on your main / second pitch
- **Break Outlier:** 99 break on one pitch and 90+ on another
- An earned quirk you pass on stays earned and shows up again next spring

**Locked until you get there**
- **Outlier I** needs 90+ velocity on your main pitch
- **Outlier II** needs Outlier I, plus 90+ velocity on your second pitch
- **Break Outlier** needs 90+ break on a pitch
- **Shortcuts:** a top-5 draft pick has a 50/40/30/20/10% chance at one locked quirk, and the top college programs can offer one

**No surprises**
- Every offer lists what each quirk does, in plain words, before you choose (some quirks have a small downside; it says so)
- Tap one for the full detail, then lock it in, pick something else, or pass for the year
- Up to 15 quirks; two-way players get a heads-up when they're close
- Same offer if you reload: no rerolling
