**On the mound**
- **Your sleeve shows:** the pitcher wears the compression sleeve he has on, with its design, instead of the plain sleeve the game puts on every pitcher
- **Your colors:** the sleeve uses the Main Color you set under Equipment Style, Sleeves & Tape

**In the menus**
- **Equipment Style preview:** the preview shows the sleeve design too, so you can see the colors while you set them
- **Clubhouse and menu previews:** players show the sleeve they have on

**What stays the same**
- Fielders already wore their own sleeve and still do
- In cold games (below about 40°F for most players) the game puts a long undershirt on the arm, and it hides the sleeve; this mod leaves the weather rule alone
