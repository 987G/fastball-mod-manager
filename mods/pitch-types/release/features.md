**Every pitcher is their real self**
- **Their real pitches:** each pitcher throws the pitches they really throw, and how often by batter side and count
- **Their real shapes:** each pitch has their own break as seen from their hand, scaled to real MLB movement
- **MLB locations:** pitch locations follow where MLB pitchers really throw
- Based on each pitcher's real 2026 season

**Their own delivery**
- **Real release point:** they let go of the ball where they really do (within about 0.1 ft in testing)
- **Real spot on the rubber:** they stand where they really do
- **Their own slide step:** every 2026 pitcher gets one, in place of the game's four shared ones
- **Kept, not swapped:** each pitcher keeps the delivery they already have, with their own release edited in
- **Player editor:** every pitcher's edited delivery is listed there, so you can give it to any player
- **Hop-Step:** a new delivery in the player editor list, "Hop-Step (Pitching++)": a Carter Capps style hop off the rubber before the front foot lands, releasing about 8 ft out

**Ratings and perceived speed**
- **Ratings still count:** movement ratings follow real MLB movement, so ratings changes in franchise modes still matter
- **Perceived velocity:** a pitcher who releases closer to the plate gives the batter less time, as in real MLB

**Pitch speed** (was Real Pitch Speed)
- **Real MLB speed at +5:** the Fastball and Offspeed Pitch Speed sliders (Settings, Sliders) reach real pitch speed at +5
  - On the game's own +5, a 98 mph fastball takes 1.1 to 1.4 times its real flight time
  - With Pitching++ a fastball takes about 0.38 to 0.43 s on screen (MLB: 0.384 s)
- **Even steps:** from 0 to +5 each pitch moves in even steps from the game's own speed to real speed; -5 to 0 stay exactly as the game made them
- **Faster pitches hit harder:** each mph of pitch adds about +0.2 mph of exit velocity, up to 111 mph (the game counts about 0.1 and stops at 100)
