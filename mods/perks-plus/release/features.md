**Umpire perks**
- **Borderline pitches go your way:** the 1-inch edge band used to be moved against you; now it moves for you by the same inch
- Covers batting perks (Green Light, Insider Info, Defibrillator) and pitching perks (Battle Tested, Theft Detection, Corner Painter, Pulse Pounder, Conductor, Filthy Break)

**Perks that did nothing or never fired**
- **Better Jump When Stealing** works: +0.03 jump score per point, +0.15 at a full stack
- **Fore! and Top Floor:** level 3 fires when the game is tied or you are losing, and each level says what fires it
- **Brick Wall and Hose Piece** can be unlocked
  - Brick Wall: 500 career putouts
  - Hose Piece: Arm Strength 80
